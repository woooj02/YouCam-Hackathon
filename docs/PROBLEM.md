# The problem, in sourced figures

Small productions dress a whole cast from a shared rack, a hire house and secondhand finds. This page collects published figures on what that costs. Every number below is copied from the source named next to it; nothing is estimated or averaged here.

**How these were checked.** Found by web search, then each figure was read on the live page (or PDF) on 2026-11-01 and corrected where the page differed. Sources that could not be opened are listed under Gaps, not in the tables. Rates are as published by each organisation and may change; currencies and years differ and are not converted.

## 5. What this supports, and what it doesn't

- **Supported:** hire is priced per piece and per period, so every extra piece and every extra week costs money; quote-only packages make comparison slow; mistakes found at the fitting cost real fees (extra weeks, alteration charges, replacement value). Small groups lean on borrowed and owned stock.
- **Not supported by anything found:** how often fittings force a re-plan, or how many hours a designer spends planning a cast. ENCORE makes no claim about either.
- **The demo prices** in the Gatsby and Midsummer showcases are fictional. The hire fees used ($20–70 a piece) sit inside the published range in section 1; they are not quotes from any supplier.
